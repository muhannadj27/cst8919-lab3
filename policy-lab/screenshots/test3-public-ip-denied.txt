TEST CASE 3: Block Public IPs (Deny-Public-IP)
=================================================
Tested in an isolated scratch resource group (rg-cst8919-lab3-isolation-test, North
Central US) with ONLY the Deny-Public-IP policy assigned, isolating it from the
region-lockdown policy for the same reason described in test2 and the README.

Command:
  az network public-ip create -g rg-cst8919-lab3-isolation-test -n pip-cst8919-test2 -l northcentralus --tags ProjectName=CST8919Lab3

Note: the resource was correctly tagged and in an otherwise-permitted region -
the only reason for denial is the resource type itself (Microsoft.Network/publicIPAddresses).

Result: DENIED

ERROR: (RequestDisallowedByPolicy) Resource 'pip-cst8919-test2' was disallowed by policy.
Policy identifiers: [{
  "policyAssignment": {
    "name": "Deny-Public-IP (isolation test)",
    "id": "/subscriptions/<sub-id>/resourceGroups/rg-cst8919-lab3-isolation-test/providers/Microsoft.Authorization/policyAssignments/pip-policy-isolation-test"
  },
  "policyDefinition": {
    "name": "Deny-Public-IP",
    "id": "/subscriptions/<sub-id>/providers/Microsoft.Authorization/policyDefinitions/Deny-Public-IP",
    "version": "1.0.0"
  }
}]

Conclusion: The Deny-Public-IP policy correctly blocks creation of
Microsoft.Network/publicIPAddresses resources regardless of tagging or region
correctness, satisfying the "no public IPs" governance requirement.

(First attempt without waiting for policy propagation succeeded and the public IP
was immediately deleted - new Azure Policy assignments can take a few minutes to
propagate to all resource providers before enforcement begins. After a short wait,
the retry was denied as expected.)
