TEST CASE 1: Region Lockdown (Only-CanadaCentral)
====================================================
Command:
  az storage account create -n stcst8919lab3region -g rg-cst8919-lab3 -l northcentralus --sku Standard_LRS --tags ProjectName=CST8919Lab3

Note: North Central US was used in place of East US as the "wrong region" example.
This Azure for Students subscription has a built-in, subscription-wide region
restriction (policy assignment "sys.regionrestriction") that only permits deployments
to: mexicocentral, westus3, norwayeast, northcentralus, southcentralus. East US is not
among them, so North Central US was used instead to demonstrate the same scenario
(a region other than Canada Central). The ProjectName tag was included so this test
isolates the region policy specifically, ruling out the tag policy as the cause.

Result: DENIED

ERROR: (RequestDisallowedByPolicy) Resource 'stcst8919lab3region' was disallowed by policy.
Policy identifiers: [{
  "policyAssignment": {
    "name": "MapleTech Secure Foundation",
    "id": "/subscriptions/<sub-id>/resourceGroups/rg-cst8919-lab3/providers/Microsoft.Authorization/policyAssignments/MapleTech-Secure-Foundation-Assignment"
  },
  "policyDefinition": {
    "name": "Only-CanadaCentral",
    "id": "/subscriptions/<sub-id>/providers/Microsoft.Authorization/policyDefinitions/Only-CanadaCentral",
    "version": "1.0.0"
  },
  "policySetDefinition": {
    "name": "MapleTech Secure Foundation",
    "id": "/subscriptions/<sub-id>/providers/Microsoft.Authorization/policySetDefinitions/MapleTech-Secure-Foundation",
    "version": "1.0.0"
  }
}]

Evaluation detail:
  expression: "location"
  expressionValue: "northcentralus"
  targetValue: "canadacentral"
  operator: "NotEquals"
  result: "True"  -> deny effect triggered

Conclusion: The Only-CanadaCentral custom policy correctly denied a resource
deployed outside Canada Central, even though the ProjectName tag was present
(confirming the region policy is what triggered the denial, not the tag policy).
